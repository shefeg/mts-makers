our story

How it all began

We met at a friend's barn dance in the Hudson Valley, both hiding from the line dancing. Noah asked if Ava knew the steps; she didn't, so they talked until the band packed up. Five years, one shared garden and a lot of tomato plants later, he asked the bigger question at the gate of that same farm.

good to know

Travel & stay

Getting there

Fly into Albany or Newburgh, or take the train from New York City to Hudson. The farm is a 25-minute drive from Hudson station.

Where to stay

We hold rooms at two inns close to the farm. Mention our names when you book by July 15.

The Mill House InnRiverside Lodge

Parking & shuttle

Free parking at the farm. A shuttle leaves Hudson station at 3:00 PM on Saturday and brings everyone back at 11:00 PM.

what to wear

Garden formal

Suits or dresses in soft colors, and shoes that are happy on grass: the ceremony is on the lawn. Evenings in September can be cool, so bring a layer.
