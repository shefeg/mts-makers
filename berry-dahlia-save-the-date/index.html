    About this demo. This page shows the Berry & Dahlia Garden save the date website template with a fictional sample couple. The template itself is a one-page Canva website with the same sections and words, edited and published in a free Canva account. In the demo, the address form keeps your answer in this browser only and sample buttons tell you what they open; on a couple's own site, the form is a Canva form and the addresses arrive in their own Canva account. The photo Home layout shows a real stock photo from Pexels; in the template it is an empty photo box for your own photo. The flowers, leaves and berries are original artwork made for this template.
