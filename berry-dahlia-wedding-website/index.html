our story

How it all began

We met at a fall potluck in Asheville, both hovering near the dessert table. Julian had brought a dahlia from his grandmother's garden to put on the table; Clara asked what it was called, and they talked until the host turned off the porch lights. Four Octobers and one very full garden later, he asked the bigger question among the dahlias.

good to know

Travel & stay

Getting there

Fly into Asheville (AVL), about a 30-minute drive from the gardens. Rideshares and rental cars are easy to find at the airport.

Where to stay

We have room blocks at two inns close to the gardens. Mention our names when you book by September 1.

The Pinecrest InnLaurel Ridge Lodge

Parking & shuttle

Free parking at the gardens. A shuttle leaves the Pinecrest Inn at 3:00 PM on Saturday and brings everyone back at 11:00 PM.
