    About this demo. This page shows the Evergreen Vellum save the date website template with a fictional sample couple. The template itself is a one-page Canva website with the same sections and words, edited and published in a free Canva account. In the demo, the address form keeps your answer in this browser only and sample buttons tell you what they open; on a couple's own site, the form is a Canva form and the addresses arrive in their own Canva account. The paper and linen textures, the olive sprig, the wax seal, the olive-grove picture, the schedule sketches and the opening video are artwork made with AI tools from our own prompts; the layout, the glass drawings and the icons are made in code.
