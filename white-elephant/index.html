✨ Step 1 & 2 Quick AI Helper

Make Together Studio Card Maker

Turn the dice chart and twist cards from your AI chat into a printable PDF, in our design.

1 In your AI chat, click Copy on the box with your cards. No box? Send the AI: Put all cards in one code block.
2 Paste it below and click Make my cards.
3 Check the preview, then click Print / Save as PDF.
Printing tip: choose Actual size / 100% scale. To get a PDF file, pick Save as PDF as the printer. Cardstock works best for the twist cards. Cut along the dashed lines. Cards in German or French? Ask your AI for LANG: de or LANG: fr; the card labels switch too.
