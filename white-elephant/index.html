Printing tip: choose Actual size / 100% scale. To get a PDF file, pick Save as PDF as the printer. Cardstock works best for the twist cards. Cut along the dashed lines. Cards in German or French? Ask your AI for LANG: de or LANG: fr; the card labels switch too.
