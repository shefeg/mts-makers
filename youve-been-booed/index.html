Boo Note Maker

Turn the poems from your AI chat into printable notes, signs and tags, in our design.

1 In your AI chat, click Copy on the box with your notes. No box? Send the AI: Put everything in one code block.
2 Paste it below and click Make my notes.
3 Check the preview, then click Print / Save as PDF.
Printing tip: choose Actual size / 100% scale. To get a PDF file, pick Save as PDF as the printer. Cardstock works best for signs. Cut along the dashed lines; fold desk signs along the middle line.
✅ Pasted!
