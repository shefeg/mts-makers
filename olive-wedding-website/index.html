what to wear

Garden Formal

Think linen suits, flowing dresses and colours borrowed from an olive grove. The ceremony is on grass, so block heels and flat shoes are your friends.

good to know

Travel & Stay

Getting there

Fly into Florence or Pisa. The villa is a fifty-minute drive through the hills; a shuttle leaves Florence at 2:30 pm on Saturday.

Where to stay

We hold rooms at two places five minutes from the villa. Mention our names when you book before June 1.

Locanda del BorgoHotel Le Mura

While you're here

Wine tasting in Chianti, the market in Greve on Saturday morning, and sunset from San Gimignano.

When should I RSVP?

Can I bring a plus-one?

If your invitation says "and guest", absolutely.

We love your little ones, but this is an adults-only celebration.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking at the villa, and a shuttle from Florence.

Will it be outdoors?

The ceremony is in the olive grove; dinner is on a covered terrace.

Can I take photos?

Please keep the ceremony unplugged; after that, snap away!
