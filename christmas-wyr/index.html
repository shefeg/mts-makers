Make Together Studio Card Maker

Turn the would you rather questions from your AI chat into printable cards, in our design. Cards in English, German or French.

Make it in 3 steps

  1. 1
    Copy the AI prompt & open ChatGPT

    Using Gemini or Claude instead? and paste it there.

    Couldn't copy automatically. Tap and hold in this box → Select All → Copy:

  2. 2

    Answer ChatGPT's questions, then tap Copy on its answer.

    No Copy button? Ask it: Put it all in one code block.

  3. 3

    iPhone: Print → Share → Save to Files. Android: Print → Save as PDF.

🔒 Nothing you type leaves your device. Your text is only kept in this browser, so it's still here if you switch apps.

Tap and hold in the box → Paste, then tap Make my cards.

1 In your AI chat, click Copy on the box with your cards. No box, or the cards opened in a document window? Send the AI: Put all cards in one code block.
2 Paste it below and click Make my cards.
3 Check the preview, then click Print / Save as PDF.
Printing tip: choose Actual size / 100% scale. To get a PDF file, pick Save as PDF as the printer. Thick paper or cardstock works best. Cut along the dashed lines.
