Halloween Bingo Maker

Turns your AI chat's list (or your own words) into printable bingo cards in our design. Every card is different, and every square is on the caller sheet.

Make it in 3 steps

  1. 1
    Copy the AI prompt & open ChatGPT

    Using Gemini or Claude instead? and paste it there.

    Couldn't copy automatically. Tap and hold in this box → Select All → Copy:

  2. 2

    Answer ChatGPT's questions, then tap Copy on its answer.

    No Copy button? Ask it: Put it all in one code block.

  3. 3

    iPhone: Print → Share → Save to Files. Android: Print → Save as PDF.

🔒 Nothing you type leaves your device. Your text is only kept in this browser, so it's still here if you switch apps.

Tap and hold in the box → Paste, then tap Make my bingo cards.

1 In your AI chat, click Copy on the box with your bingo list. No box? Send the AI: Put the whole bingo list in one code block.
2 Paste it below and click Make my bingo cards. No AI? Pick a ready set, or type your own words in the form.
3 Check the pages, then click Print / Save as PDF.
Change the details or type your own words (no AI needed)
Printing tip: choose Actual size / 100%. To get a PDF file, pick Save as PDF as the printer. Your words stay on this computer: nothing is uploaded. The same list always makes the same cards, so a reprint matches the caller sheet; Mix new cards makes a new set. Markers: small things (candy, cereal, coins) are a choking hazard for children under 3; little ones use crayons.
