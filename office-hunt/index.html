No AI? Make a quick hunt from your own hiding spots

Type your hiding spots in order, one per line. The prize waits at the last one. The Card Maker writes code and scramble clues itself (and uses our ready riddle when a spot matches one of our 24 office spots). Teams: every team gets the same spots in its own order and the same prize spot.

Printing tip: choose Actual size / 100% scale. To get a PDF file, pick Save as PDF as the printer. Thick paper or cardstock works best. Cut along the dashed lines.
