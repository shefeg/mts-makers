    About this demo. This page shows the Fine Ink & Blush save the date website template with a fictional sample couple. The template itself is a one-page Canva website with the same sections and words, edited and published in a free Canva account. In Canva the opening is a still envelope section with an Open button, which you can delete; this demo animates it. Add to calendar opens Google Calendar with the sample date filled in. The address form here keeps your answer in this browser only; on a couple's own site the form is a Canva form and the addresses arrive in their own Canva account. The illustrations were made with an AI image tool from our own prompts; the layout is made in code.
