    About this demo. This page shows the Emerald Vow save the date website template with a fictional sample couple. The template itself is a one-page Canva website with the same sections and words, edited and published in a free Canva account. In the demo, the address form keeps your answer in this browser only and sample buttons tell you what they open; on a couple's own site, the form is a Canva form and the addresses arrive in their own Canva account. The garden gate, flowers and seal are artwork made with an AI image tool; the frames, divider and layout are made in code.
