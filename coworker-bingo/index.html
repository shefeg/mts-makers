Find the Coworker Bingo Maker

Turns your coworkers' fun facts (typed in the form, or from your AI chat) into printable "find someone who…" bingo cards in our design. Every card is different.

Make it in 3 steps

  1. 1
    Copy the AI prompt & open ChatGPT

    Using Gemini or Claude instead? and paste it there.

    Couldn't copy automatically. Tap and hold in this box → Select All → Copy:

  2. 2

    Answer ChatGPT's questions, then tap Copy on its answer.

    No Copy button? Ask it: Put it all in one code block.

  3. 3

    iPhone: Print → Share → Save to Files. Android: Print → Save as PDF.

🔒 Nothing you type leaves your device. Your text is only kept in this browser, so it's still here if you switch apps.

Tap and hold in the box → Paste, then tap Make my bingo cards.

Change the details or edit the squares
Printing tip: choose Actual size / 100%. To get a PDF file, pick Save as PDF as the printer. Your words stay on this computer: nothing is uploaded. The same list always makes the same cards; Mix new cards makes a new set.
For personal use in your home, at your own party or office party, or in one classroom; please don't resell or share the files.

Fun facts form: no AI needed

Type each coworker's name and the fun fact they chose to share, written so it follows "Find someone who…" (for example has met a penguin up close). Leave out the name inside the fact. We mix your facts with our squares, make every card different, and print a secret answer key for you. Only use facts people were happy to share.
