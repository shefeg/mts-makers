Mahjong Score Sheet Maker

Type your club, players and house rules once: they go on every page of your Christmas score book. Then print or save as PDF.

Make your score book in 3 steps

1 Fill in what you need below. Every field is optional: blank fields print as lines to write on.
2 Click Make my pages and check the preview at the bottom.
3 Click Print / Save as PDF. Choose Actual size / 100%.
🔒 Nothing you type leaves this device: no account, no upload. Your form is kept only in this browser, so it is still here next time. Clear everything removes it.

Your hand log (optional)

This Maker comes empty: it includes no hands from any card. If you like, type the hands you want to track from the card you own, for your own use. Please don't share or sell a filled-in log: the hands on a card belong to its publisher.

Print

Ink-saver prints the same pages in black and white with outlines only (no coloured fills or backgrounds), so it uses far less ink.
To get a PDF file, pick Save as PDF as the printer. Phone: Print → Share → Save to Files (iPhone) or Print → Save as PDF (Android).
For personal use and for your own club or group: print as many copies as your group needs. Please don't resell or share the files.
