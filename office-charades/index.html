Make Together Studio Card Maker

Office Christmas Charades · turn the cards from your AI chat into a printable PDF, in our design.

1 In your AI chat, click Copy on the box with your cards. No box, or the cards opened in a document window? Send the AI: Put all cards in one code block.
2 Paste it below and click Make my cards.
3 Check the preview, then click Print / Save as PDF.
Printing tip: choose Actual size / 100% scale. To get a PDF file, pick Save as PDF as the printer. Thick paper or cardstock works best. Cut along the dashed lines.
✅ Pasted!
