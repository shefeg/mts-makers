good to know

Travel & stay

Getting there

Fly into San Francisco or Oakland. The orchard is 20 minutes from Sonoma Plaza: use the gate on Orchard Lane, not the farm road your GPS may suggest.

Where to stay

We hold rooms at two inns in town. Book on the inn's own website or by phone, and mention our names by April 15.

The Plaza InnVine Street Lodge

Parking & shuttle

Free parking 400 m west of the gate. A shuttle leaves Sonoma Plaza at 3:15 PM on Saturday and brings everyone back at 11:00 PM.

what to wear

Garden party

Light suits and summer dresses in soft colors, and shoes that are happy on grass: the ceremony is in the orchard. Evenings cool down, so bring a layer.

Questions & answers

What time should I arrive?

The ceremony starts at 4:00 PM. Please arrive by 3:30 PM to find a seat.

What should I wear?

Garden party: light suits, summer dresses and shoes that are happy on grass.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones; this is an evening for adults.

Can I bring a guest?

Your invitation names everyone we have saved a seat for.

Where do I park?

Free parking 400 m west of the gate, or take the shuttle from Sonoma Plaza.

Do you have a registry?

Your presence is our gift. If you'd like more, our registry is just below.

Can I take photos?

The ceremony is unplugged; after that, please snap away.

What if it rains?

The ceremony moves into the Long Barn. Evenings cool down, so bring a layer.
